Find your people at the reception

Guests give their name, bride-or-groom and friend-or-family – everything else is optional. The app draws a live graph with you in the middle and every other guest placed by how much your answers overlap – so you know who to sit next to, dance with, or wave at.

Interactive prototype – answers are real inputs, the 22 guests are fictional. Open on a phone for the full-screen view.
